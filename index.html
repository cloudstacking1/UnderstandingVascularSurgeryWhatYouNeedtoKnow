Common Vascular Conditions We Treat

  • Peripheral Venous Disease (PVD):

    • A condition where veins in the legs cannot pump enough blood back to the heart, leading to swelling, pain, and varicose veins.

  • Peripheral Artery Disease (PAD):

    • Occurs when arteries become narrowed or blocked, reducing blood flow to limbs and increasing the risk of heart attack and stroke.

  • Varicose and Spider Veins:

    • Enlarged veins that can cause discomfort and are often a cosmetic concern.

  • Aneurysms:

    • Abnormal bulges in blood vessels that can be life-threatening if they rupture.
